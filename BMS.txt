A fully working blog management system (BMS) is a software application that allows users to create, edit, organize, and publish digital content.
Here are the key features and functionalities of a complete system, divided into core modules:
1. User Authentication and Roles
• Registration & Login: Secure sign-up and sign-in using email, passwords (hashed), or social logins (OAuth).
• Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Distinct permissions for visitors, registered authors, and administrators.
• Profile Management: Users can update their display name, bio, profile picture, and change passwords.
2. Content Management (CRUD Operations)
• Create Posts: Rich-text or Markdown editors to write titles, bodies, excerpts, and format text.
• Media Uploads: Ability to upload, crop, and insert images, videos, or documents into posts.
• Read & View: Publicly accessible interface to read published articles, view author info, and see publish dates.
• Update Posts: Edit existing drafts or published articles.
• Delete Posts: Soft or permanent deletion of unwanted articles.
3. Organization and Taxonomy
• Categories: Broad groupings to classify posts (e.g., Technology, Lifestyle).
• Tags: Specific keywords for precise post-filtering and indexing (e.g., Python, Budget Travel).
• Search Functionality: Real-time search bar to look up posts by keywords, tags, or titles.
4. Editorial Workflow & Publishing States
• Drafts vs. Published: Save work in progress without making it public.
• Scheduled Publishing: Set a future date and time for automatic posting.
• Archiving: Hide older posts without deleting them from the database.
5. Engagement and Interaction
• Comment System: Registered users (or guests) can leave feedback or replies on posts.
• Comment Moderation: Admins or authors can approve, edit, or delete inappropriate comments.
• Likes / Reactions: Simple counter or heart icon for readers to appreciate posts.
6. Administration and Dashboard
• Admin Control Panel: High-level overview of total views, post counts, user numbers, and comments.
• User Management: Admins can ban users, promote members to editors, or reset credentials.
• SEO Management: Custom meta titles, descriptions, and URL slugs for search engine optimization
